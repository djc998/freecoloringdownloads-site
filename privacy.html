Privacy policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

freecoloringdownloads.com is a website for parents, teachers and other adults to download free printable coloring pages. You don't need an account, and we don't ask for your name, email or any other personal information.

What we collect

Like most websites, our hosting provider (Google Firebase) records standard technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request. This is used to run and secure the site and is not used to identify you. We also keep a count of how many times each page has been downloaded.

Cookies and advertising

To keep the pages free, we show ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalized advertising in Google's Ads Settings, and learn more in how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Ads on pages with children's artwork are requested as child-directed, which means Google does not show personalized ads or remarketing on those pages. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before any cookies are used for advertising.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit, which pages are popular and which pages get downloaded, so we can make more of what people like. It uses cookies and collects information such as the pages you view, the pages you download, whether you follow a link to the Color Kingdom app, your approximate location (country or city) and your device type. We do not use it to identify you, and Google's advertising features (Google Signals and ad personalization) are turned off for Analytics. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent first, and Analytics does not use cookies for them unless they agree. You can also opt out with Google's opt-out browser add-on.

The Color Kingdom app

This policy covers this website. The Color Kingdom app for iPhone and iPad has its own privacy policy, which explains the app's ads, analytics and purchases: colorkingdom.app/privacy.

Children

This site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to codepoetlabs@gmail.com.

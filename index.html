Frequently asked questions

Are these coloring pages really free?

Yes. Every coloring page is free to download and print for personal, family and classroom use. There's no sign-up and no account.

How do I print a coloring page?

Open any coloring page and choose “Download & print PDF”. The PDF is US Letter size and prints on any home or school printer; choose “Fit to page” for A4 paper.

Can teachers print copies for a class?

Yes. You can print as many copies as you need for your classroom, homeschool group or party. Please don't sell the pages or repost the files on other websites.

Are there coloring pages for adults?

Yes. The mandala and floral collections have detailed, relaxing designs made for teens and grown-ups, alongside simpler pages for younger kids.

How often are new coloring pages added?

New pages are added every day, including seasonal collections such as Halloween.

Can I color the pages on an iPad or iPhone?

Yes. Every page is also in Color Kingdom, our free coloring app for iPad and iPhone, with Apple Pencil support.
